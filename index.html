Usually within a week. If we're mid-sprint on something, we tell you up front.

Project-based, fixed scope. Projects land anywhere between $200 and $15k — a quick landing page sits at the low end, custom apps and AI work at the top. Exact quote after the brief call.
Do you handle design too?
Yes. Design and dev under one roof. If you've already got a designer, we're happy to work with them too.
Can we just hire you for one thing?
Yep. Web, automation, or AI — pick your fighter. No bundled upsells.
Where are you based?
India. Remote-first, async-by-default. Available across time zones for the calls that matter.
